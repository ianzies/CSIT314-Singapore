What ALIGN does

Clearer hiring, cleaner matching.

ALIGN is a recruitment platform designed to make job discovery and candidate shortlisting more structured. Candidates build detailed profiles, employers post role requirements, and the system ranks matches using skills, education, experience, work mode, and location.

01

For Candidates

Create a profile, browse jobs, filter opportunities, and view ranked job recommendations based on your background and preferences.

02

For Employers

Build a company profile, create job postings, search candidates, and view recommended candidates for each hiring need.

03

Membership Access

Members receive unlimited ranked recommendations, while non-members can view the top 10 matches.
